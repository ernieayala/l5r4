Miscellaneous Equipment

There is a variety of equipment commonly found in the hands of samurai and other castes beyond weapons and armor.
Backpack: Rokugani backpacks are built upon wicker frames, making them fairly rigid. Price: 3 zeni
Ball, Kemari: A leather ball used to play kemari, a popular game. Price: 5 zeni
Baskets: The most common container, crafted from wicker. Sometimes worn as hats. Price: 1 to 5 zeni
Blacksmith's Hammer: Necessary for an armorer or weaponsmith to craft. Price: 3 bu
Blanket: A coarse blanket appropriate for travel, often used stacked for warmth. Price: 1 bu
Bottle of Bleach/Dye: Used primarily for clothesmaking and hair coloration. Price: 1 bu
Bottle of Sake: Rice wine, a very popular beverage among bushi. Price: 1 bu
Bottle of Shochu: A very strong, distilled alcoholic beverage. Price: 2 bu
Bottle, Empty: Clay or occasionally imported glass, used for storing liquids for travel. Price: 1 zeni
Books and Scrolls: Sturdy records used for journals and recording new information. Price: 1-10 bu
Bowyer's Kit: Used for maintenance of bow and arrows. Price: 2 bu
Brazier: Vessel for holding burning substances or for heating liquids. Price: 1 bu
Bucket: A metal vessel with a handle, primarily toting water. Price: 1 zeni
Candle: A simple, hand-dipped candle. Price: 1 bu
Chest: Wooden chest with a lock for storing precious items. Price: 2 bu (wood), 8 bu (metal)
Chopsticks: Eating utensils. Price: 1 zeni
Cloth, Silk Bolt: Used for mending, protective wrapping, etc. Price: 2 koku
Coin Purse: A small, easily concealed bag. Price: 3 zeni
Daisho Stand: Easily disassembled and carried, this is a stand for displaying one's daisho and tanto. Price: 1 koku
Dice and Dice Cup: Used primarily for gambling. Price: 25 zeni
Divination Kit: Sets of coins or carved sticks used for divining. Price: 1 bu
Finger of Jade: Rare, and used to ward off the Shadowlands. Price: 1 koku if it can be found at all.
Fishing Kit: Consists of a hook, strong string, a small net, and a cleaning knife. Price: 10 zeni
Flint and Steel: Useful for creating sparks and lighting fires. Price: 1 zeni
Furoshiki Sack: A sturdy silken bag with a drawstring. Price: 1 zeni
Games, Traveling: Small sets of go, shogi, or other games. Price: 1 bu and up
Grapple Hook: An iron hook meant to be attached to a rope for climbing. Price: 1 bu
Kiseru: A simple smoking pipe. Price: 3 zeni
Kubi Bukuro: A net bag for holding and displaying the severed head of an enemy. Price: 2 zeni
Lanterns: Used for decoration and illumination. Price: 1 bu (paper), 4 bu (metal)
Lantern Oil: A flask with two hours of oil. Price: 3 zeni per flask
Lucky Cricket: A small cricket in a cage. Price: 4 bu
Medicine Kit: Needle and thread, cloth bandages, herbal disinfectants (10 uses). Price: 1 bu and up
Mirrors: Glass or metal, polished to a high sheen. Price: 3 bu to 2 koku
Mortar & Pestle: Used to grind and mix ingredients. Price: 2 bu
Musical Instruments: Common samples include the samisen, biwa, flute, and drum. Price: 1 to 5 bu
Palanquin: Elaborate personal carriage, carried by servants. Price: 10 koku
Paper: Ideal for writing for scrolls or "books." Price: 1 bu per 5 sheets
Parchment & Charcoal: Cheaper alternative to brush and ink. 3 zeni for 10 of each
Personal Seal or Chop: Used to sign formal documents and verify identities. Price: 4 bu
Pet: Dog, cat, songbird, monkey, or others. Incapable of combat. Price: 1 koku
Pillow Book: Small, loosely bound book about romance, adventure, or poetry. Price: 3 zeni to 1 koku
Pot, Iron: Used for cooking while traveling. Price: 15 zeni
Quiver: Cloth satchel for arrows (holds up to 60). Price: 20 zeni
Rope: Varying quality made of hemp, hair, or silk. Price: 5 zeni per foot of reasonable quality rope
Sake Cup: Guests often bring their own cup to events. Price: 1 bu
Shovel: A simple digging tool. Price: 5 zeni
Small Back Banner: Identify name, rank, family, and Clan. Price: 1 koku
Small Folding Stool: Carried by soldiers to conserve strength. Price: 3 bu

Small Knife: Used for carving or personal grooming. Price: 5 zeni
Small Painting or Sculpture: Carried for sentiment or gifts. Price: 3 bu
Small Statue of Fortune/Ancestor: Carried for sentimental reasons or luck. Price: 1 koku
Spices: Used to add flavor to food while traveling, or for barter. Price: 1 to 5 bu for small bottle
Sweets: Popular snacks include small cakes, candies, buns, or honeyed rice. Price: 1 bu for 4 servings
Tatami Mat: Used for sitting or sleeping on the ground or floor. Price: 2 zeni
Tattoo Needles: Made of steel or bamboo. Price: 1 bu per set
Tea Set: Necessary to perform the sacred tea ceremony. Price: 1 koku
Tent, Chomchog: A large and elaborate tent favored by the Unicorn. Price: 20 koku
Tent, Small: Suitable for two people. Price: 1 koku
Tent, Yurt: Smaller than the chomchog but still larger than average, favored by the Unicorn. Price: 10 koku
Traveling Rations: Dried rice or fish, wrapped in paper. Price: 5 zeni per meal
Umbrella: Made of wicker and silk to hold off the elements. Price: 2 bu
Walking Stick: A simple cane to aid in walking. Not suitable for a weapon. Price: 1 zeni
Whetstone: Used to sharpen weapons, but not the katana or wakizashi. Price 1 zeni
Writing Box: The sumi-e box, contains brushes, ink stones, a water bowl, and sand. Price: 1 bu
Clothing & Accessories

Cap, Courtier's: Peaked cap popular among courtiers and bureaucrats. Price: 2 bu
Fan, Courtier's: Paper fan with customized patterns. Price: 40 zeni and up
Hakama: Knee-length trousers. Price: 75 zeni
Haori: Jacket-like garment worn over a kimono. Price: 25 zeni
Kataginu: Vest-like garment worn over kimono to emphasize shoulders. Price: 40 zeni
Kimono: The basic robe worn by all samurai. Comes with belt/sash (obi). Price: 1 bu and up
Makeup Kit: Face powders and paints. Price: 1 koku
Mask: Vary from silk to metal (mempo). Often intricately patterned. Price: 3 bu
Netsuke: Jewelry such as earring, ring, bracelet, etc. Price: 1 bu

Obi Pouch: A small pouch that fastens to the belt. Price: 25 zeni
Perfume: Made from various herbs and common substances. Price: 2 bu per small bottle
Sandals: The simplest and most common form of footwear. Price: 50 zeni
Snow Shoes: Wide-framed shoes to prevent sinking in the snow. Price: 2 bu
Traveling Cloak: Thick coat worn over clothes while traveling. Price: 75 zeni
Wide-brimmed Straw Hat: Used for protecting the face from the sun. Price: 1 zeni
War Paint, Vial: Popular among Crab, Unicorn, and Lion warriors. Price: 3 bu
Wig: Used for disguise or to gain attention in court. Price: 1 bu each
